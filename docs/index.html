AbioticTrackIR

6-DOF Head Tracking & Off-Axis Parallax 3D Perspective Mod for Abiotic Factor. Turn your monitor into a holographic glass window into GATE Cascade!

Mod.io Showcase Quick Setup Guide

🌟 Key Features

Full 6-DOF Freedom

Full 3-axis rotation (Pitch, Yaw, Roll) and 3-axis translation (Lateral lean, Surge depth, Heave height).

Parallax 3D Frustum

Dynamically computes asymmetrical off-axis projection frustums coupled to your physical head position on normal 2D monitors.

OpenTrack & TrackIR

High-speed shared memory (FT_SharedMem) & non-blocking UDP (Port 4242). Supports webcams, phone cams, IR clips, and TrackIR 4/5.

Independent Head-Look

Head tracking naturally steers your view and perspective while mouse controls weapon aiming and crosshair reticle.

Decoupled Free-Look

Hold Left Alt to look around freely 360° while maintaining body motion and tool orientation.

Plug & Play Proxy

Direct version.dll proxy hooking without patching game executables or modifying core game archives.

🎮 Default Hotkeys

Hotkey Action Description
F12 Recenter View Zeroes current head position as the center forward view.
F11 Toggle Tracking Enable or disable head tracking instantly.
F10 Toggle Parallax 3D Toggle off-axis asymmetrical perspective frustum.
Left Alt Free-Look Hold to look freely decoupled from character body.

🚀 Quick Setup with OpenTrack

  1. Start OpenTrack on your PC.
  2. Choose your tracker input (e.g. neuralnet for webcam, or PointTracker for IR LEDs).
  3. Set Output to freetrack 2.0 Enhanced (Recommended) or UDP over network (Port 4242).
  4. Click Start in OpenTrack.
  5. Launch Abiotic Factor and press F12 to calibrate!
